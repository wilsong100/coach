1. Ankle & Lower Leg Activation
Ankle Circles & Alphabet: 1 set per foot to ensure full range of motion.

Bent-Knee Calf Raises: 15 reps per side to target the soleus muscle, which absorbs the most force during high-speed running.

Pogo Hops: 2 sets of 20 seconds. Focus on "springy" ankles and minimal ground contact time, mimicking the turnover needed for your 3:47/km best pace.

2. Lower Back & Core Bracing
Bird-Dogs: 10 reps per side. This is a staple in your Workout B and is critical for stabilizing the spine before explosive speed.

Cat-Cow Stretch: 10 reps to gently mobilize the vertebrae.

Deadbugs: 12 reps to "switch on" the deep core, protecting the lower back from the high impact of 3:50/km intervals.

3. Running-Specific Dynamics
A-Skips & B-Skips: 20 meters each. These drills reinforce the high cadence and proper foot strike seen in your Garmin data.

Leg Swings (Forward & Lateral): 10 per leg to open the hips and reduce pull on the lower back.

High Knees: 2 sets of 15 seconds to prepare the heart rate for entering Zone 5.
